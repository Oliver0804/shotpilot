ShotPilot 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 17 日

ShotPilot 是把手機當成虛擬攝影機、在 3D 場景裡運鏡的工具。我們重視你的隱私：App 不蒐集、不儲存、也不傳送任何個人資料給我們或任何第三方。

我們不蒐集的資料

  • 沒有帳號、不需要登入。
  • 沒有廣告、沒有分析或追蹤工具。
  • 不存取相機、麥克風、照片、聯絡人或位置。

動作感測器

App 讀取陀螺儀與加速度計來計算手機的角度與高度變化，用來控制虛擬鏡頭。這些資料只在手機上即時運算；連線到你自己的電腦時，才會透過區域網路送到那台電腦上的 ShotPilot 桌面版，不會上傳到網際網路。

區域網路

App 會請求「區域網路」權限，只用來連線到你在同一個 Wi-Fi 下執行的 ShotPilot 桌面版（傳送鏡頭姿態、接收即時畫面）。你輸入的電腦 IP 位址只存在手機本機。

本機儲存

在示範模式錄製的運鏡只存在 App 執行期間的記憶體中；設定（例如電腦位址）只存在手機上。刪除 App 即會一併刪除。

兒童隱私

App 不蒐集任何人的個人資料，包括 13 歲以下的兒童。

聯絡我們

有任何問題，請到 GitHub Issues 留言。

ShotPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

ShotPilot turns your phone into a virtual camera for moving through 3D scenes. The app does not collect, store, or send any personal data to us or to any third party.

What we don't collect

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Motion sensors

The app reads the gyroscope and accelerometer to work out the phone's orientation and height changes, which drive the virtual camera. This data is processed on the phone in real time. When you connect to your own computer, it is sent over your local network to the ShotPilot desktop app on that computer only. It is never uploaded to the internet.

Local network

The app asks for Local Network access solely to connect to the ShotPilot desktop app running on the same Wi-Fi (sending camera pose and receiving the live view). The computer address you enter is stored only on your phone.

Local storage

Camera moves recorded in demo mode stay in memory while the app is running. Settings such as the computer address are stored only on your phone and are removed when you delete the app.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Contact

For questions, please open an issue on GitHub.
